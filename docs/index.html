The Purpose

Why we built this.

Most bulk tools today are either bloated paid subscriptions or buggy abandoned scripts. We wanted something better: A professional, open-source engine that just works.

The Common "Paid" Experience

  • Slow, clunky interfaces with too many ads.
  • Server timeouts when creating more than 10 pages.
  • Broken Gutenberg block support.
  • Hidden costs and monthly subscription traps.

The WBPG Masterpiece

  • Crisp, Geist-inspired UI for maximum focus.
  • Async Sequential Engine: No timeouts, ever.
  • 100% Block Compatible out of the box.
  • Permanently free and open-source craftsmanship.
Core Features

Crafted for Reliability.

Zero-Lag Injection

Our engine uses DocumentFragments and virtual DOM techniques to ensure that even if you're injecting 100 rows, your browser stays fast and responsive.

Accident-Proof

Built-in lock detection. We prevent you from accidentally refreshing or navigating away during a critical batch job.

Hierarchy Support

Easily nest pages under parents to build deep SEO silos or service directories in a single operation.

Accessible for Everyone

Built to WCAG 2.2 AA standards. Keyboard navigable menus, focus visibility, and screen reader support come standard.
